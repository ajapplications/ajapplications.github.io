Privacy

Privacy policy.

This policy covers the AJ APPLICATIONS website. Individual products may have their own, more specific data practices noted on their product pages.

Website data

This website does not require an account to browse. Contact form submissions include only the information you choose to provide.

Product-specific data

Data practices differ by product. NEXAVAULT is designed to keep files on infrastructure the owner controls. Castrix, as an account-based product, stores account and listening data needed to operate the service. Not all AJ APPLICATIONS products share the same data practices, and each product page notes what applies to it.

Cookies

The website may use minimal, functional cookies such as a stored theme preference. No advertising cookies are used on this website.

Analytics

Any analytics used are limited to understanding general site usage and are not used to build advertising profiles.

Third-party services

Some products integrate third-party infrastructure where relevant — for example, Castrix's administration workflow uses the Telegram Bot API. Any such integration is disclosed on the relevant product page.

Data retention

Data is retained only as long as needed to operate the relevant product or respond to a contact request.

User rights

You can request details about, or deletion of, data associated with your use of a specific AJ APPLICATIONS product by reaching out through the contact page.

Changes

This policy may be updated as products change. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact page.
