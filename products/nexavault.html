Development story

Why NEXAVAULT exists

NEXAVAULT started from a simple frustration: most cloud storage means trusting a company with your files indefinitely. NEXAVAULT is built so the storage layer, the authentication, and the audit trail all run on hardware the owner actually controls — a home server, a small box on a local network, or a self-managed machine.
