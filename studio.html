Studio

Built from scratch.

AJ APPLICATIONS is an independent software studio focused on original software and experimentation. Products are designed and written from the ground up rather than assembled from templates or existing platforms.

Original Code

Build from scratch rather than simply re-skinning existing products. Every flagship product in the ecosystem — from NEXAVAULT's storage layer to AJ.Live's streaming pipeline — is written specifically for that product.

Real Problems

Software should solve actual problems: a place to store files without handing them to a third party, a way to talk to strangers without an account, a stream player that doesn't depend on someone else's platform.

Privacy

Consider privacy and data control during product design. NEXAVAULT runs on hardware the owner controls; NovaTalk is built around anonymous sessions rather than persistent accounts.

Experimentation

Not every experiment needs to become a commercial product. Projects like AJ Search or TarabTendo exist because they were worth building and learning from, not because they had a business plan attached.

Engineering

Projects should be technically interesting as well as useful — a fixed-timestep game loop, a local RAG pipeline, an RTMP-to-HLS pipeline are all engineering problems worth solving properly.

How the studio works

One person, many disciplines.

AJ APPLICATIONS operates as an independent studio rather than a traditional company. Products move through distinct stages — experimental, in development, beta, and live — and that status is always shown honestly rather than smoothed over for marketing.
